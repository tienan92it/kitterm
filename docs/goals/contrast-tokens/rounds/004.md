## Effects

- **Row actions keep their hover, and the cue is the appearance.**
  `.quiet` and `.more` gain a 1 px inset `--ui-border` ring **on hover
  only**. The crew's argument is that a hover cue may appear on hover and
  the appearance is itself the state. The fill against the card stays at
  1.053; the ring reads 1.439 against the card and 1.366 against the
  fill, and 1.530 and 1.403 on `gruvbox-dark`.
- **It also fixes a case the fill could never carry.** Inside an open
  `.menu`, which paints `--ui-surface-2` itself, the hover fill reads
  **1.000**: the row action was invisible on hover there before round 3
  as well. The ring is the whole cue in that case.
- **Scoped, not blanket.** The rule is `button.quiet`, because
  `.tally.quiet` is a span, and a line of text must not outline under the
  pointer.
- **The selection toolbar needed a stronger colour, and the crew showed
  why.** `--ui-border` cannot carry the pressed state: it reads 1.183

  what the press had already lost. So `.selection-action:active` takes an
  inset `--ui-accent` ring, at 8.792 against the bar and 7.614 against
  the fill on `github-dark`, and 2.986 and 2.528 on `gruvbox-dark`, its
  worst theme. `--ui-accent` is already this page's interaction colour,
  in the focus outline and `.launch:hover`.

## Reflection

Round 3 found the rule and missed three of its consequences. Round 4
found them because a reviewer enumerated every rule that paints one of
the four tokens and asked, for each, what cue was left. That is a
mechanical question and it caught what judgement had missed.

The useful part is that the crew did not apply round 3's recipe blindly.
It measured `--ui-border` against the pressed fill, found 1.183, and said
plainly that the recipe does not work there before choosing a different
colour. A repair round that repeats the earlier round's answer without
re-measuring would have shipped a second invisible state.
