## Effects

- behaviour: `PullRequestStatus` starts `gh` with `posix_spawn` in its
  own process group on the serial queue `kitterm.pulls`, with a 20 s
  timeout that sends SIGTERM and then SIGKILL to the group. One thread
  polls both pipes and reaps the process. The answer per pull request is
  `{number, title, state, draft, headRefName, mergedAt?, url, ci?,
  additions, deletions}`, with `ci` reduced to `failing`, `pending` or
  `passing`. A failed read keeps the last good list and its `readAt`
  and adds the reason. The login check is `gh auth token` with its
  output sent to `/dev/null`. The minute counts from the end of a read.
  `gh` is found on the daemon's `PATH`, then `/opt/homebrew/bin`,
  `/usr/local/bin` and `~/.local/bin`, absolute entries only.
