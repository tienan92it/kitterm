## Reflection

The item was a removal and the crew treated it as one: it took out the
form and everything that existed only to serve it, rather than leaving
`hasLiveAgent` and the `ModelRow` fields behind as dead weight for
capability 5 to trip over. 166 px came off the page at both widths, and
that is before the foundation lands.

One fact for `facts.md`. A Playwright full-page capture taken right
after a 4 s wait rendered the strip 390 px below its place once, while
`getBoundingClientRect` showed the DOM correct. A second capture of the
same page was right. **Check a full-page capture against the box tops
before trusting it** — a screenshot that lies is worse than no
screenshot, because a round ships it as proof.
