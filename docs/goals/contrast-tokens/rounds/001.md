## Reflection

The round changed the shape of the goal. `KNOWN_BELOW` was a 5-line note
about a few dim labels; it is now a 273-entry measurement of a page that
misses the floor on every theme. Capability 3 wants that table empty, and
capability 2 can reach only 29 of the 39 failing pairs. The plan was
written against the hand list, and the hand list was wrong by a factor of
nine.

The cost a rule can prevent is none here; the round did exactly what it
was chartered to do, and the size of what it found is the finding. What
this shows is worth keeping: **a hand-written list of what to check is a
claim about coverage, and the first round that derives the list instead
should expect the claim to be wrong.**
