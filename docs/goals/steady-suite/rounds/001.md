## Effects

- **One helper, twelve sites, no assertion touched.** The foreman checked
  the diff for removed assertions and found none, and `grep` finds zero
  bare `waitUntilExit()` left outside the helper and its own test.
  `XCTestCase.waitForExit(of:within:output:file:line:)` polls `isRunning`
  at 10 ms, and on expiry fails at the caller's `#filePath` and `#line`
  with the test name, the command, the pid, and the tail of the daemon's
  `server.log`, then `SIGKILL`s the stall.
- **The deadline is 30 seconds, chosen from measurement.** The crew ran
  the seven process-spawning classes, 47 tests, three times: 43.3, 41.3
  and 41.3 seconds, no failures, and **every exit wait finished inside
  one 12.7 ms poll**, including a `serve` with two shells taking
  `SIGTERM`. The daemon's stop path is bounded at 150 ms. 30 s is over
  two thousand times the measured maximum, and the crew said which way
  it weighted: a false red on a swapping box or a loaded runner costs a
  whole rerun, while a loose deadline costs 30 s once per stall instead
  of the job's own timeout.
- **A thirteenth cannot arrive.** A test reads the test sources and fails
  on a bare call, naming file and line. The crew planted one and quoted
  the failure:
  `PidAfterBindTests.swift:103 waits without a deadline; use waitForExit(of:)`.
- **The stall failure reads well.** Quoted from a deliberately stalled
  process:
  `` `sh -c printf 'stalling…' >> "$KITTERM_STATE_DIR/server.log"; exec sleep 300` (pid 32911) did not exit within 1.0 s; last output: stalling on purpose ``,
  reported at the call site.
- **Two targets share one file without touching `Package.swift`.** The

## Two things the round found that the foreman did not know

**`Tests/` does not compile on Linux at all**, and did not before this
round: `MCPSendKeysTests` uses `URLSession` without importing
`FoundationNetworking`. CI runs only `swift build` there, so the Linux
gate has never covered the test target. Every "the Linux build caught a
`Sendable` error" in the last four rounds was a `Sources/` error; a
Tests-only one would pass unnoticed.

**The plan's floor line was wrong.** It said vitest is 1328 at the start;
this tree counts 1214, and its `Web/` is byte-identical to `main`. The
1328 was the count before `fleet-catch-up` round 4 removed seven colour
pairs. The foreman wrote the plan from a stale number the same morning it
changed.

## Reflection

Three rounds met this hang and each wrote it down as somebody else's
problem. It cost eighteen minutes, eighteen minutes, and thirty-seven
minutes of CI before a round owned it, and owning it took one helper and
a source check. The cost of recording a defect instead of fixing it is
visible here in minutes.

The measurement is the part worth keeping. The crew did not guess the
deadline: it timed 47 tests three times, found every wait finishing in
one poll, and picked a bound two thousand times larger on purpose, with
the reason written down. A deadline chosen that way will not be argued
with later.
