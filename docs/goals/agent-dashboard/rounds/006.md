## The correction inside the round

The crew built all four panels, measured, and **stopped rather than
shipping past the goal's own completion condition**. The four panels add
671 px at 390 px, which put the page at 1873 against a condition of
1200, and capability 5's 47 px does not cover it. It asked.

The foreman's call, which the crew applied: **fold `WHERE` and `MODELS`
at 390 px behind the fold the page already has**, closed by default,
with the figure in the summary line — `where the dollar goes · 91%
unattributed`, `by model · Fable 5.1 $1,388.50`. `VALUE` does not fold:
what the spend bought is the one measure that earns its height on a
phone. Nothing folds at 768 px and up.

The reasoning came from the foundation rather than from convenience: it
already drops `LEAKS` below 768 px because a panel is a measure, and
`WHERE` and `MODELS` are the same kind of thing and the two largest.

A stylesheet cannot open a closed `details`, so the fold is built in JS
on `matchMedia("(max-width: 767px)")` with a repaint on crossing the
breakpoint.

## The VALUE divisor, and why it is not the research's number

A unit cost divides **the spend of the checkouts counted**, not the
fleet's. `kitterm` reads $11.41 a PR, $0.016 a line and $43.15 a release
beside the research's $11.77, $0.017 and $40.70 over a range one day
apart. The model hour divides the fleet's measured dollars, $56.01, and
the note says so in one line.

That is the right divisor: dividing the whole fleet's $2,458 by
kitterm's own counts gives $28.25 a PR, which is a number about nothing.

## Reflection

Two things in this round are the loop working.

The crew stopped at the completion condition instead of shipping past it
or quietly relaxing it, and it stopped with a measurement and three
options rather than a question. That is the difference between a round
that needs a decision and a round that needs a rescue.

And it corrected the research that justified its own existence. The
low-cache count was off by more than half, and the crew reported it
against its own interest — the research is what argued the panel should
exist at all. A round that only confirms the plan that produced it is
not evidence of anything.
