# Request 01: the worst themes are readable

Approved 2026-09-10. Frozen.

## Fixture

A scratch daemon with one project, two goals, three sessions, and one
pending approval, so the page shows the strip, a card with expanded and
one-line goals, chips, tallies, and a row's meta line. The theme is set
in turn to `synthwave-84` and `solarized-dark`, the two the reviews
measured lowest.

## Request

Open `/sessions` at 1200 px and at 390 px on each theme.

## Expected behaviour

Every line of text is legible against what it sits on. The measured
ratios for the meta line, the chip text, the tally counts, the state
labels, and the strip's detail line are at or above 4.5:1 on both
themes.

## Expected persistent effects

None. The screenshots are the record.
