  asked.** Part 1's footer answered the round in one line —
  `001 files not counted: the Result: line names no sha`. The sha
  pattern required at least one digit, and roughly one short sha in a
  thousand is `a`–`f` alone (`dcaacec`). The parser read a real sha as
  no sha and printed a dash for a diff git could have counted.
- behaviour: `GoalLedger.parse` now reads a hex word of 7 to 40
  characters as a sha, preferring one with a digit when a line holds
  both, so a word like `deadbeef` beside a real sha never wins.
  `filesChanged` returns `FilesChanged` — a count, `noSha(line)`,
  `notRun`, or `failed(range, status, stderr)` — with stderr captured;
  `kitterm goal cost` prints one footer line per unexplained dash and
  `filesChangedReason` in `--json`. The table on success is unchanged.
- visible: `testAShortShaWithNoDigitIsStillASha` pins the fixture's
  first commit at `@1700000531`, which is `dcaacec`: three failures
