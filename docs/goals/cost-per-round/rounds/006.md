## Reflection

The fix stops new damage. A pane already damaged keeps the wrong join
until its own `claude` sends a hook from its project; that hook is
accepted, and the stranger's next hook is refused. Limits, with no code:
two hooks judged at once against one join can both record; a `realpath`
on a dead mount blocks that hook's task, not the loop; a pane at a
registered parent of several checkouts accepts a hook from any of them;
the refused set and the verdict cache do not ride a live upgrade; a pane
with no join takes a stranger's first hook. A later round can decide
whether a join expires when its `claude` exits.
