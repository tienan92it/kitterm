## Correction

One correction, inside the round. The crew reported the field at 12 px
and named the limit itself: iOS Safari zooms the page on focus of a
field under 16 px and leaves it zoomed. The crew offered a viewport
`maximum-scale=1` as the alternative. The foreman rejected that one: it
stops pinch-zoom for every reader on every browser to fix a focus
behaviour on one, which is an accessibility loss the goal's exclusions
forbid. The crew set `.reply-input` to 16 px, the one size on the page
above 13, and measured the result at 390 px: body height 1326, the same
as before; `scrollWidth` 390; in all three forms the field and its
`[send]` share one line. The 16 px text fits inside the 44 px the tap
target already held, so nothing else moved.
