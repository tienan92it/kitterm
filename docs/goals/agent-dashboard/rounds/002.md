## Why the phone page grew

**+50 px at 390, and it is the foundation's own rule.** Every
interactive target is 44 px there: the header went 24 to 44, the usage
toggle rows 24 to 44, the push switch 24 to 44, the card head 36 to 44.
`--space-5` replacing a 64 px bottom padding gave 40 back. At 1200 px
every control is 28 and the page fell 54 px.

That is the trade the foundation names and it is not a regression: a
44 px tap target is WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8, and capability 5 takes the height
back by making every line one line.

## Reflection

The crew found a defect the design had not: `.notice` broke principle 5
before the principle existed, and measuring rather than assuming caught
it. It also caught its own mistake the same way — it wrote
`light-dark(dark, light)` first, and the mark rendered the light hex,
which only measuring the **page** rather than the sheet revealed.

Three facts for `facts.md`: `light-dark()` takes the light value first;
`theme-contrast-derive` reads `tokens.css` only, so a page-scoped
override is invisible to it; and a turn's findings belong in a note
before the last step, because the crew that posts them may not survive
to finish.
